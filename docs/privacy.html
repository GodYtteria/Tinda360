Privacy Policy

Effective for Tinda 360 (Android)

Tinda 360 is an offline-first point-of-sale (POS) and inventory app designed for Philippine sari-sari stores. Your store data belongs to you.

1. Local On-Device Storage

All products, sales, utang records, and store settings are stored locally on your Android device in an SQLite database. Tinda 360 does not run analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party telemetry.

2. On-Device AI Assistant

Smart Store Assistant answers questions by reading your store's local database directly on your device. No store queries or financial figures are sent to external AI servers.

3. Optional Google Sign-In & Google Drive Backup

If you choose to connect your Google account in Onboarding or Settings, Tinda 360 requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This allows the app to:

  • Identify your signed-in Google account email for display in Settings.
  • Upload and restore your encrypted/compacted store database backup (tinda360_backup.sqlite) inside your own private Google Drive App Data folder.

Tinda 360 cannot read, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive. We do not operate any backend servers and never receive or store your Google Drive data.

4. Camera Permission

Camera access is used solely on-device when you open the barcode scanner to look up or add products.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete any store database inside Settings, disconnect your Google account at any time, or uninstall the app to remove local data.

6. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact officialtinda360@bukluran.com.
